HOW WE WORK

From first review
to informed decisions.

A structured origination process. KS Capital prepares and coordinates the opportunity; the lending or investment decision always rests with the capital provider.

01Documents & discoveryKS CAPITAL
We understand the funding purpose and request corporate documents, financial statements and other relevant information.
02Preliminary screening & DDKS CAPITAL
We review the business, financial performance, cash-flow visibility, debt capacity, security and key risks. Material information gaps are flagged early.
03Financing fit & structuringKS CAPITAL
We assess appropriate facility types and prepare credit analysis, indicative transaction structures and supporting financial information.
04Selective lender & investor matchingKS CAPITAL
We prepare a clear submission and approach suitable third-party lenders or private credit investors whose mandates align with the opportunity.
05Investor's go / no-go decisionCAPITAL PROVIDER
The capital provider independently decides whether to advance to detailed due diligence or decline the opportunity. A decline may end the process or require restructuring.
06Full lender due diligenceCAPITAL PROVIDER
If interested, the lender conducts its own due diligence and credit review. KS Capital coordinates information requests and communication as appropriate.
07Term sheet & executionCAPITAL PROVIDER
Subject to satisfactory review, the capital provider may issue a term sheet. Final terms, credit approval, legal documentation and disbursement remain subject to its independent process.
